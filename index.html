The Case for Change

One platform to replace a stack held together by hand.

Today Lam Clinic runs on BigCommerce + Rebillia + spreadsheets + back-end impersonation checkout, with no true point of sale and no single source of truth. The migration consolidates all of it onto Shopify Plus before the BigCommerce contract expires in April 2027 and the second location opens in Q4 2026.

01

62% of orders are assisted checkout

Medical assistants impersonate patients in the BigCommerce back end to charge card-on-file. Shopify POS Pro + draft orders replaces this with true assisted selling across clinic locations.

02

Subscriptions are leaking revenue

Renewal success sits at 74.2% and dunning recovery at 8.1% on Rebillia. A modern subscription app targets 85–95% renewal and 20–40% recovery — a core "why now."

03

Scaling from 2 toward 50 locations

A second location opens Q4 2026. Shopify Plus multi-location, unified reporting, and a Plus Organization support the multi-brand model without a rebuild.

App Add-Ons Discussed

The stack we've talked through.

Every add-on below came out of the discovery and technical assessment conversations. Native Shopify Plus capabilities (Multipass SSO, checkout extensions, Shop Pay Installments) carry no separate app fee.

Subscriptions · Must-have

Recharge

~$99/mo + usage — migrate off Rebillia

Aligned choice for auto-ship. Candidates also reviewed: Loop, Stay AI, Smartrr, Appstle. Selected for dunning/recovery strength, card-updater, Authorize.Net→Shopify token migration tooling, and POS/assisted-checkout compatibility. Highest-risk migration item (~940 plans / 401 subscribers) — partner-led with a pause-billing window.

Marketing · Must-have

Klaviyo (email + SMS)

~$720/mo at ~40K profiles

Reliable automated refill reminders, reorder flows, and win-back — the automation that was hard to maintain on BigCommerce. Deep native Shopify integration.

Accounting · Keep in place

QuickBooks connector

~$100/mo

QuickBooks Enterprise stays as the accounting system of record; a connector syncs orders, payouts, and financials from Shopify.

Inventory · Fast-follow

PO / sourcing (Stocky or IMS)

Stocky included with POS Pro (included in Plus)

Replaces spreadsheet-based stock across 473 SKUs. Native multi-location inventory at launch; a PO app or dedicated IMS for reorder points and lead times as a fast-follow.

Migration · One-time

Matrixify

One-time during migration — not a monthly cost

Bulk import of products, full order history, and ~40K customers (non-PHI only). Cancelled once cutover is complete.

Growth · Nice-to-have

Reviews · Loyalty · Upsell

~$139/mo combined

Reviews/social proof, loyalty/rewards, and cross-sell/upsell. Well served by the Shopify app ecosystem — prioritized after the core migration and in-clinic workflow are proven.

Included in Shopify Plus — no extra app fee: POS Pro at every location, Multipass single sign-on (collapses the three-identity problem in Phase 2), checkout UI extensions for the required Provider/Coach attribution field, Shop Pay + Shop Pay Installments, and unified online + POS + subscription reporting in one admin.

Why Shopify Plus

What the monthly spend buys.

  • Single source of truth — online, POS, and subscriptions in one admin, replacing BigCommerce + Rebillia + spreadsheets + a separate supplement portal.
  • True assisted checkout — POS Pro + draft orders replace back-end impersonation for the 62% of orders that are in-clinic, without slowing the card-charge step.
  • Healthier subscription revenue — modern retry logic and card-updater target 85–95% renewal (from 74.2%) and 20–40% dunning recovery (from 8.1%).
  • Built to scale — multi-location and Plus Organization support the 2 → 50 location plan and the Phase 2 multi-brand model without a replatform.
  • Compliant by design — Canvas Medical stays the PHI system of record; Shopify holds non-PHI commerce data only, with the integration built and validated by the partner in Phase 2.
